About FundSprout

FundSprout is a fully offline student finance companion. All data is stored only in this browser's local storage — nothing is sent to a server, and there are no accounts or sign-ins.

On overspending: FundSprout allows a purchase to bring your balance below zero, but will always ask you to confirm first. This mirrors real-world spending and keeps the app flexible if you log an expense before its matching allowance.

Version: 1.0 · Built with HTML, CSS, Bootstrap 5.3, vanilla JavaScript, and Chart.js. No backend, no build tools, no tracking.
